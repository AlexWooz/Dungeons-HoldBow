Hold Bow for Minecraft Dungeons (v1.17.0.0, Steam)
==================================================

Keeps your ranged weapon in hand after you shoot, like Minecraft Dungeons II,
and you can walk around with it. Your next melee attack switches back to the
melee weapon, which then stays out until your next shot.

Tested with the Bow, Longbow and Exploding Crossbow.

REQUIREMENTS
- Dungeons Blueprint Loader 1.0 (Blueprint-Loader.pak) in the same ~mods folder.

INSTALL
1. Copy HoldBow.pak into:
   <Steam>\steamapps\common\MinecraftDungeons\Dungeons\Content\Paks\~mods\
2. Start the game.

CONTROLS
No new keys. Shoot as usual to take out the ranged weapon. A melee attack
(A on controller, Left Mouse on keyboard and mouse) switches back. Clicking
to walk keeps the ranged weapon in hand.

Works together with the Third-Person Camera, Analog Movement and Jump mods.

UNINSTALL
Delete HoldBow.pak from ~mods. There is no in-game toggle.
